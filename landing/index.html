05 How it works

A four-step loop —
invisible while you write.

i
You write.
An email, message, script, or draft answer. PersonaFlow reads it the moment you pause.
ii
AI listens.
Tone, emotion, personality signals, and conversational context are extracted in milliseconds.
iii
It suggests.
Quietly. A refined version, a tone alternative, a strength, a sharpening — never a demand.
iv
You decide.
Final voice is always yours. PersonaFlow assists. You author.

07 Our principles

Communication AI
that respects who you are.

An AI that shapes how humans speak to each other carries an unusual responsibility. These commitments aren't marketing — they're the architecture.

i.
Assist, never author.
PersonaFlow suggests. You choose. The final voice is always yours — we do not send, post, or speak on your behalf without explicit action.
ii.
Privacy is foundational.
Conversations are end-to-end encrypted. We never train models on user messages. Enterprise tenants get full data isolation and on-device options.
iii.
Refuses manipulation.
We do not generate deceptive, coercive, or dark-pattern communication. The system declines requests that bend persuasion into manipulation.
iv.
Audits its own bias.
Tone models are evaluated across gender, culture, language, and dialect — and re-audited on every release. We publish what we find.
